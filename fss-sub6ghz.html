A Miniaturised High-Pass Frequency Selective Surface for Sub-6 GHz Shielding Applications

P. Choubey, M. J. Akhtar

Abstract: This paper investigates an ultra-low-profile, highpass frequency-selective surface (FSS) for sub-6-GHz shielding applications. The FSS exhibits high pass behaviour with the -10 dB threshold at 5 GHz with unit cell size of 4.86 mm. A 0.2mm FR4 substrate provides mechanical stability to the single layer structure. A parametric study demonstrates tuning of the high-pass cut-off frequencies while maintaining the same unit-cell periodicity. The design was analysed in Ansys HFSS

Keywords: Frequency selective surfaces (FSS), electromagnetic shielding, sub-6 GHz, metasurfaces, microwave engineering, electromagnetic compatibility (EMC), miniaturization.
